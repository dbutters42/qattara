# 01 — BRIEF

**Project name: Qattara** *(working title, chosen 2026-08-25)*

---

## 1. The name

After the **Qattara Depression Project** — a real, repeatedly-proposed scheme to flood a basin in Egypt's Western Desert with Mediterranean seawater. The depression bottoms out around 133 m below sea level; proposals to cut a canal or tunnel to the sea and generate hydroelectric power date back to John Ball in the 1920s, resurfaced in the 1970s, and were studied again as recently as 2023. It has never been built.

It's a good name because it *is* the game: a low place, a wall of land between it and the water, and someone wondering what happens if you cut through.

**Design touchstone:** the first scenario should probably be exactly this — a dry basin, a ridge, a sea on the other side, and a digging tool. Worth building as the tutorial-that-isn't-a-tutorial.

Two details from the real project that the simulation already models, and that are worth leaning into: evaporation is the thing that undermines the scheme, and the resulting lake would grow steadily saltier. The sim has an evaporation pass from day one. Salinity is a plausible later addition.

## 2. The vision

A large simulated sandbox the player digs around in. Generated topography of rock and soil. Water that flows and collects downhill. The player modifies the topography — freeing water, redirecting it, damming it. No ending objective; the fun is in the playing.

Seen from above, god-view, no player character. Google Maps terrain view that you can edit.
